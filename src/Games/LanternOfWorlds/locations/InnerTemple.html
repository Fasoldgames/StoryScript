You enter into a large, square hall.

The hall has a floor of dark marble and a high, arched ceiling.
The walls are of a dark stone, and from them, lifelike sculptures seem to emerge, as if naturally growing from the walls.
The sculptures are of hooded, winged figures, which appear to able to spring alive at any moment.

In the centere of the hall, there is a square, slighly raised platform, with a majestic looking statue on top of it.
