The ancient ruins lie before you. Silent and still.
The remains of an old temple, to an even older deity whose name had fallen into oblivion.

The ruins are scarred and burned. There appear to be old bloodstaines on the stones, and the marks of blades and arrowheads on the broken pillars.
There has been fighting here, and a lot of it.
'No wonder the villagers can't sleep at night', you think.
'Sounds and lights, they had said. Eerie cries in the night. And a hundred gold if you and Henrick could give them back their nights rest.

They also said the unrest has been increasing for several days, as if something is stirring within the ruins. Somewhere deep within.

'Probably just a lot of superstitions,' you think.
'Some wolves or bears who made the ruins their new home. Should be easy money.'
'Henrick,' you shout.
'I'm going to do some scouting. Won't be too long.'
The old man, who is juist building a fire, looks up grumpily.
''Okay. But stay close by, and don't go rushing in, like last time. Some scouting only. Give me time to prepare some things.'

'Pfff', you think.
Who needs preparations. When you are a mighty adventurer such as yourself.
You raise your hand in acknowledgement, and start walking towards the ruins.

You feel the familiar thrill. The excitement of coming adventures and looming battles. Who knows what dangers you will find,
what enemies you will face, and what treasures you will uncover!

It's very quiet around you, the only thing you hear is the occasional bird sound, the rustling of the wind through the leaves, and Henrick some distance behind you, preparing the camp.

As you look around, you notice a small, tomb like structure at the far end of the ruins. The structure seems largely intact, with a stone door that has been slightly cracked, but still seems very sturdy.

Also, you notice something sticking out of the earth, near one of the burned pillars.
