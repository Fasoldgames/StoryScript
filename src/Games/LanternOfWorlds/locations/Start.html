After a long voyage, across forest paths and stoney trails, you have finally arrived at the ancient ruin. And not a moment too soon.

You feel a stinging pain in your buttocks. The result of the wagon rocking about, and the hard wooden bench on which you had to sit for hours, and maintain your balance.

Old man Henrick didn't seem to mind, or notice, and kept a stern hand on the reigns, and a sharp eye on the distant road. The elderly man continues to surprise you.
One of these days, you will have to ask him more about himself, and why exactly he has offered his help, and guidance. And keep pressing for answers.

But for now, a quest lies ahead, and preparations are in order. You jump off the wagon and start to stomp around, to bring back some life to your sleeping legs.
Henrick climbs off too, and frees the horse from it's reigns. Then, he starts to make camp for the night.
'We'll start first thing in the morning' Henrick says, without looking at you.
'Too late in the day to do something now.'

You glance at the sky. There is still plenty of time, you think. Plenty of time to do some scouting and exploring.

As you return to Henrick and the wagon, you see the old man has made a crackling fire, and has set up his cooking pot above it.
An appetizing scent reaches you from the cooking pot.

The old man is busy stirring the contents of the pot with a long wooden laddle, and now an again adds some herbs from a pouch at his belt.

Henrick gives you a silent nod as you approach, and continues his cooking.
