After a long voyage, across forest paths and stoney trails, Brennus had finally arrived at the ancient ruin. And not a moment too soon.

He felt a stinging pain in his buttocks. The result of the wagon rocking about, and the hard wooden bench on which he had to sit for hours, and maintain his balance.

Old man Henrick didn't seem to mind, or notice, and kept a stern hand on the reigns, and a sharp eye on the distant road. The elderly man continued to surprise Brennus.
One of these days, he would have to ask him more about himself, and why exactly he had offered his help, and guidance. And keep pressing for answers.

But for now, a quest lay ahead, and preparations where in order. Brennus jumped off the wagon and started to stomp around, to bring back some life to his sleeping legs.
Henrick climbed off too, and freed the horse from it's reigns. Then, he started to make camp for the night.
'We'll start first thing in the morning' Henrick said, without looking at Brennus.
'Too late in the day to do something now.'

Brennus glanced at the sky. There was still plenty of time, he thought. Plenty of time to do some scouting and exploring.

As you return to Henrick and the wagon, you see the old man has made a crackling fire, and has set up his cooking pot above it.
An appetizing scent reaches you from the cooking pot.

The old man is busy stirring the contents of the pot with a long wooden laddle, and now an again adds some herbs from a pouch at his belt.

Henrick gives you a silent nod as you approach, and continues his cooking.
