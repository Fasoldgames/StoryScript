The structure seems to have been surrounded once by a much larger room or hall, and served as it's center. But the surrounding hall
or room has been reduced to nothing but rubble.
You think the structure might have been something special, granting entrance to an inner sanctum of sorts, reserved only for
high priests or clergy of a certain status.

The stone door of the structure seems very stury, and there does not seem to be a handle or a keyhole. With great effort, you manage to find enough grip for your fingers, and enough strength, to force the stone doors open!
The doors open with a heavy grinding sound.

You are greeted with a puff of stale air escaping, and a musty scent. For a moment, the scent brings imagesto mind of the
passing of eons, and a vast, motionless ocean of time.

In the darkness beyond the doors, you can make out stone steps, leading down.
