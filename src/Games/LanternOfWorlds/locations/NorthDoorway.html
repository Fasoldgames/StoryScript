Before you lies a short hallway, with at the end, a large double door.

The door is made of sturdy oak, reinforced with steel, and seems to have suffered little from the passage of time.
There are no handles or locks on the door, but there are several runes and symbols chisseled into the wood.
At the centre of the door there are two round indentations. One in each door.

It seems as though something can be placed into the indentations.
