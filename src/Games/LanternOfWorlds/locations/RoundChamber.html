As you descend the stairs, you enter into a large, rounded chamber.

The chamber is lit by several torches, that seem strangely new and recently placed.

In the centre of the chamber there is a square stone pedestal. It seems the pedestal has born a statue once,
judging from the debris around it. You can see a stone hand, a part of a face, and the greater part of a torso amidst the rubble.
It appears as if the statue was toppled from the pedestal with considerable force.

As you look around further, you see three doorways in the walls of the chamber. One to the west, one to the north,
and one to the east. As you move closer, there is a sudden glittering within the debris.
It seems to be a piece of jewelry, a silver ring with a green gemstone.

The ring looks scratched and weathered, but still quite valuable.
