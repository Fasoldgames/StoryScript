As you come close to the pillar, you see what appears to be a bit of rusted chainmail, sticking out of the earth.

You partially unearth the skeleton of a long dead warrior. The skeleton is clad in a rusted, greatly decayed chainmail. In one of it's bony hands, it holds the broken hilt of a sword.

Your eyes are drawn however, to a strange emblem, fasted to the chainmail at the right shoulder. It is an emblem made of bronze, resembling a radiant sun. The emblem seems strangely untouched by the ravages of time.
