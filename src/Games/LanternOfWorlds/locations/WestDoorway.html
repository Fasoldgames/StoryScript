You enter into a small square chamber.

The chamber seems to have served as a bathing room once. There is a rectangular stone basin in the middle, filled with muddy water.
On the walls are the remains of wooden shelves, and on the floor beneath them, the shards of what must have been flasks and bottles.

At the far end of the chamber, a stone wheel portrudes from the wall. As you move into the room, a the muddy water in the basin is disturbed by a sudden ripple.
Beneath the surface of the water, a dim red glow lights up. Then, the water begins to stirr and ripple more violently.

From the water rises the skeletal remains of a man. Torn shreds of clothing hang from bleeched bones, and in the empty eye sockets
of the skull, a red light burns. In his bony hand, the skeletal man grips a rusted dagger.

With an unearthly sigh, the skeletal man attacks you!
